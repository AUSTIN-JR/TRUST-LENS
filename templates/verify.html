How Verification Works

  1. Upload Original: When you upload an image, we calculate its SHA-256 hash (a unique fingerprint)
  2. Store in Blockchain: This hash is recorded in the blockchain, creating a permanent record
  3. Verify Later: When you verify an image, we recalculate its hash
  4. Compare: If the new hash matches the stored hash → ✅ Unaltered. If it doesn't → ⚠️ Tampered

Why it works: Even changing a single pixel in an image completely changes its SHA-256 hash. This makes tampering detectable.
